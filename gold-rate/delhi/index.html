About Gold Rates in Delhi

Gold rates in Delhi, Delhi follow the IBJA (India Bullion and Jewellers Association) benchmark published twice daily. The AM rate is released around noon and the PM rate in the evening on working days.

How the Gold Rate Is Decided for Delhi

There is no separate "gold rate of Delhi" set by any local body. Instead, jewellers across Delhi price their gold against the national benchmark published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA), the apex industry body. IBJA releases its indicative rates twice every working day — an AM rate around midday and a PM rate in the evening.

Today's AM benchmark for Delhi is ₹147,662 per 10g (24K), and the PM benchmark is published later in the evening. These numbers are built from three ingredients: the international gold spot price, the USD-INR exchange rate, and applicable import duties and taxes at the wholesale level. When global prices rise overnight, the morning IBJA rate in Delhi usually opens higher; when the rupee strengthens, it cushions the rise.

Because every jeweller in Delhi references the same IBJA sheet, the base gold value is nearly identical from shop to shop on a given day. Where shops in Delhi differ is in making charges, stone-setting charges, and any small premium they add — which is exactly why comparing making charges matters more than hunting for a "cheaper gold rate" in Delhi.

  • 22K (916 purity) — ₹135,258 per 10g: 91.6% pure. The standard for gold jewellery across Delhi. If you are buying bangles, chains, or rings in Delhi, this is almost certainly the rate on your bill.

Making Charges and GST: What You Will Actually Pay in Delhi

The IBJA rate is only the starting point. In Delhi, your final jewellery bill has three parts. First, the gold value at the day's rate — for example, 10 grams of 22K at ₹135,258. Second, 3% GST, charged on the total of gold value plus making charges. Third, making charges, which generally range from about 8% to 25% of the gold value depending on how intricate the design is; machine-made chains sit at the lower end, handcrafted pieces at the higher end.

Put it together for a simple example in Delhi: a 10-gram 22K necklace at today's rate carries a gold value of ₹135,258. With 12% making charges (about ₹16,231) and 3% GST on the subtotal, the final bill lands near ₹156,034. Two jewellers in Delhi quoting the same day's gold rate can still differ by thousands of rupees on this final figure — which is why you should always ask for the rate, weight, making-charge percentage, and GST shown as separate lines on the bill.

Gold as an Investment: Physical Gold vs Sovereign Gold Bonds

At ₹147,662 per 10 grams, gold remains the most trusted store of value for households in Delhi. If you are buying for investment rather than wearing, you have options beyond jewellery. Physical gold — coins and bars in 24K — tracks the benchmark most closely and carries no making charges beyond a small minting premium, but you must arrange safe storage and it attracts 3% GST at purchase.

Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), issued by the RBI from time to time, are denominated in grams of gold and have historically paid an annual interest on top of gold-price movement, with no storage headache. Check whether a fresh SGB tranche is currently open before choosing this route. Gold ETFs and mutual funds offer another paper-gold path with high liquidity through your demat account.

A practical approach used by many buyers in Delhi: jewellery for wearing and family occasions, and coins, SGBs, or ETFs for pure investment. Spreading purchases across months — rather than betting everything on a single day's rate — smooths out the daily ups and downs you see in the trend above.
